## Triage habit to fix

The dominant repeated over-ask is **confusing a run's capabilities with the operator's authority**: “triage cannot file the issue,” “implement cannot dispatch the proof,” or “this draft needs review” becomes `needs-operator` (agentic-job#3143/#2397, tracker#353/#1071). This is a qualitative finding, not a measured per-cause census. Route the authorized controller action or record a dependency instead of asking the same engineering question again.

Require triage to name the **reserved decision**: credentials, upstream publication, rulesets, disclosure, budget, bounds, follow or target admission. Otherwise require a safe default, correctly routed plan or evidenced dependency. Missing validation is neither success nor automatically a policy question; unchanged prerequisites should not trigger another release/retriage loop.

Separately, [agent-board#724's audit](https://github.com/cgwalters-forge/agent-board/issues/724#issuecomment-6677834254) found 12 done/obsolete holds among 85 verdict holds in its 100-item sample—not this document's totals. Those hit the close-lock rule. Expose the refusal reason, keep #111 intact, and use explicit operator disposition or approved pre-triage locking, not a supervisor bypass.
